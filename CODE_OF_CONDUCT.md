# Community code of conduct

openCFW is a technical community built around careful evidence, respectful
review, and the shared goal of keeping user-owned hardware useful.

Participants are expected to:

- treat people with patience and respect, including when technical conclusions
  differ;
- critique claims and evidence without personal attacks;
- welcome contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels;
- respect privacy, consent, intellectual-property boundaries, and coordinated
  security disclosure; and
- avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sexualized attention,
  deliberate disruption, or publication of another person's private data.

These expectations apply in repository discussions, reviews, project events,
and other spaces where someone represents openCFW. Maintainers may edit or
remove harmful content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban
participants whose conduct is incompatible with this policy. Enforcement
decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and
protect the safety and privacy of affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do
not open a public issue containing personal or sensitive information. This
document is openCFW-authored documentation distributed under the root MIT
License.
