# Wave 14: touch stock RAM, em9305 size, codec SRAM alias

Final links: g2/build/g2c-w14-final-{touch,em9305,codec-a,codec-b,apollo-main,apollo-bootloader}. All six have 0 undefined symbols.

## touch
- Stock reset path (/tmp/touch.s): at Reset 0x4674, copy {0xb58c->0x200004c0, 0xf1 words} and zero {0x200008a8, 0x1ac words}, then crt0 _start at 0x3464 with sp = 0x20002000 (pool 0x34c4). _stack_init at 0x3458 sets sl = sp-0x10000, so there is no linker stack size. Nothing references a heap.
- Stack: the stock capacity is 0x20002000-0x20000f58 = 0x10a8 (4264 B). Ours is 0x20002000-0x20000f8c = 0x1074 (4212 B) because of 0x34 B of extra linker .bss. An ld ASSERT requires at least 0x10a8-0x200.
- The `.touchdata` (0x3c4 B) and `.touchbss` (0x6b0 B) regions are generated by .waves/w14/touch_gen_ram.py into g2-touch-stockram.ld.inc. That gives 17 data pins, 8 bss pins, and 27 gap bytes from stock (touch_stock_reset_gaps.c).
- Byte compare of the linked LMA 0x9ee8 against stock 0xb58c: 18 of 964 bytes differ.
  - 14 are the low halves of 7 flash pointers: pm callback, common_config, widget_config, pin_config, shield_pins, shield_electrodes, and a function. Each points to the matching object in our flash layout.
  - 4 are the deliberate zeroing of 0x2000087c/0x20000880. These are crtbegin init/fini pointers 0x3435/0x3409; we have no __libc_init_array. FLAG.
- The tables start with the stock-shaped records, then add {.data 0 words} and {.bss 13 words}.
- The "missing 7 KB" is not init data. Stock init data is only 964 B; the difference is code (stock text 0x3300..0xb58c).
- SystemCoreClock = 24 MHz (stock word 0x20000878 = 0x016e3600).
- Open: our `.noinit` input (0x20 B) is discarded by the linker. Stock 0x20000884..0x200008a8 (36 B) is not covered by either table.

## em9305
- Bloat is codegen. The 1422 matched functions are 253,742 B ours vs 167,196 B stock. Byte-identical duplicates total 2,964 B and double definitions 2,760 B. Only 608 B is provably unreferenced, and it was not removed. gc is unsound (it keeps 7 KB).
- Fix: profiles g2-em9305-1/-2 changed from -O2 to `-Os -fno-tree-loop-distribute-patterns`. memcpy is routed in g2-em9305.ld with PROVIDE(memcpy = opencfw_em9305_memcpy), the reconstruction of stock 0x332fc4.
- bin 325,624 -> 216,988 B. Note: earlier byte-match evidence was produced at -O2.

## codec (GX8002 SDK third-party/upstream/nationalchip-lvp-kws/arch/soc/grus)
- link.ld `.dummy_for_stage2_sram_text_place_hold { . += SIZEOF(.stage2_sram_text); } > stage2_dram`, together with soc_config.h's equal IRAM/DRAM sizes, shows that 0x1xxxxxxx and 0x2xxxxxxx are bus views of one SRAM. CONFIG_FLASH_XIP_BASE is 0x10200000 (base_addr.h:88).
