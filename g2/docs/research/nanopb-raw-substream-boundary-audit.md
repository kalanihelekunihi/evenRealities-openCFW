# Nanopb raw-value and substream boundary audit

## Result

The 224 opaque bytes immediately after promoted `pb_skip_field` are two exact
nanopb functions, not one undifferentiated segment:

| Stock span | Bytes | SHA-256 | Identity | Direct callers | State |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|---|
| `[0x0048F6EA,0x0048F77E)` | 148 | `1e970a9b198943e309550811010fc1ba4008d671d023abfc5c893b965fee2bc9` | private `read_raw_value` | 1 | Source-recreated and promoted (Apple profile) |
| `[0x0048F77E,0x0048F7CA)` | 76 | `db925e0c532bac2f2e38f398c7b7d99669afe4d41e6690b08116e9f06ec7d88d` | public `pb_make_string_substream` | 3 | Source-recreated and promoted (Apple profile) |

Rizin disassembly and the independent fail-closed analyzer establish both
boundaries. `read_raw_value` has one caller at `0x0048FBBE` and three calls to
the already source-owned `pb_read` entry at `0x0048F3BE`. It refers to the
16-byte `varint overflow` and 18-byte `invalid wire_type` diagnostics. A local
source adaptation can own both strings, so no opaque executable provider is
required.

`pb_make_string_substream` has callers at `0x0048F9DA`, `0x0048FB56`, and
`0x0049049C`. It calls the source-owned public `pb_decode_varint32` entry at
`0x0048F5AE` and retained compiler helper `__aeabi_memcpy` at `0x00439C04` for
the 16-byte stream copy. Its only data seam is the 24-byte
`parent stream too short` diagnostic. Promotion should either authenticate and
retain the memcpy seam explicitly or close it with a separately reviewed
source implementation; it must not silently treat compiler runtime as nanopb.
The production source chooses the latter: four named ABI-field assignments
compile to direct loads/stores and retain no `memcpy` relocation.
