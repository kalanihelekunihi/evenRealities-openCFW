## Historical residual ownership audit — 2026-11-11

All 17 functions (1,150 stock bytes) listed as unresolved in the wave 27
checkpoint now have identifiable C providers in the current Apollo manifest.
Each provider has a symbol in the strict source-linked ELF. Fifteen map to
upstream or local-vendor C in `upstream-map.tsv` (including the prior MSPI
row); two first-party clock waits now have stock-address anchors beside their
existing C implementations. The two smallest rows are Ambiq HAL's
`am_hal_read_words` wrapper at `0x48086A` and LVGL's `_out_null` callback at
`0x483030`. The older module labels for FreeRTOS, nanopb and LVGL rows were
inferences and should not override these source-level matches.

The linked `_out_null` body is `70 47`, matching the complete two-byte stock
body hash. The current Ambiq word-reader wrapper is four bytes and tail-calls
its ITCM helper, while the stock wrapper is eight bytes and calls/returns.
It therefore remains a byte-different C provider.

This audit closes the *historical 17-row source-ownership list*. It is not a
fresh whole-image function census or proof of instruction equality. Evidence:
[`apollo-historical-residual-source-owners-batch-20261111.json`](../evidence/apollo-historical-residual-source-owners-batch-20261111.json).

## Current-source correction — 2026-11-11

The historical residual list below is not a current missing-C queue. Its two
largest rows now have compiled C owners: stock INFO reader `0x4D3DDA` maps to
the local `infoX_read` in the Ambiq HAL source unit, and stock database-hash
handler `0x5346E4` maps by call sequence to Cordio's generated
`appServerHandleDbHashUpdate`. Both symbols are present in the current
source-linked Apollo ELF. This establishes source ownership, not byte
identity or completeness for the other residual rows. Evidence:
[`apollo-historical-residual-source-owners-20261111.json`](../evidence/apollo-historical-residual-source-owners-20261111.json).

Generated by `.waves/sim-fix/coverage2.py` (sim57 ELF) and `missing_strings.py`; full data in `.waves/sim-fix/{coverage,uncovered,missing_strings,stubs}.json`.

Method. A stock function in `g2/symbols/apollo_main.tsv` (8,844 rows) is matched to one of ours when (1) the tsv name is one of our ELF FUNC symbols, (2) one of our symbols carries its stock address as a `_xxxxxx` suffix, (3) a `stock 0x…` comment precedes one of our function definitions, or (4) a source file is named `*_xxxxxx.c`. The matches then propagate along the ordered BL-callee lists of matched pairs (difflib alignment, 40 iterations).

Result: 6,890 of 8,844 matched. 543 unmatched runs total 298,700 bytes. Rows larger than 64 KB are excluded; tsv row 0x442134 (FUN_005fa120) claims 1.8 MB and is bogus.

**Unmatched does not mean unported.** Most unmatched rows are unnamed library internals (LVGL, FreeType, littlefs, nanopb, tlsf). We build those from upstream source, where they get inlined or static and differ in size from IAR's, so call lists don't line up. The checks that do show real gaps all come back clean:

- **Raw stock-code pointers in our sources** (flashreloc rescan, sim57): the only live one was `display_driver_uled_transfer_async.c` `0x004c072d`, now fixed to `mspi_dummy_callback` (Fix 51). The rest are justified: the npmx ops table (not read; our upstream npmx_adc does its own conversion), `0x4b2df9` (a faithful stock byte-table overrun, data only), and the constants (nanopb field info, image w/h, VTOR base, NEMA command words, hash bytes).
- **Undefined symbols:** 0 in the final link. The composed list is 24, and those 24 names are identical to sim53: linker-script symbols, 3 IAR putchar hooks, and nrf_fuel_gauge, which is a prebuilt library.
- **Stubs:** among matched pairs where stock is larger than 32 bytes and ours is `bx lr` or `movs r0,#n; bx lr`, there is exactly one: `silent_mode_refresh_callback` (stock 0x469470). Stock returns 1 on every path, so it is equivalent. Our functions of size 0 or 4 are asm symbols with no `.size`, or tail-call thunks.
- **Strings:** 45 unmatched functions reference strings that are absent from our image. All are LVGL/littlefs assert paths or texts (asserts are compiled out) or log text that our port rewords (`quicklist_convert_58da28.c`, `display_flush_lock_47381e.c`). None of them is missing logic.

The range 0x55e99c..0x55f74c, which I earlier called "unported", is the stock nPM1300 npmx driver. Our build has it from upstream source (`npmx_adc_*` at 0x5b5c6c..0x5b62d4, `npmx_main_driver.c`). Its tsv rows are just unnamed. I corrected the cell comment.
