Correction to the earlier bounded semantic review: `__iar_zero_init3` does not safely clear arbitrary nonzero byte lengths. Stock subtracts four and writes one word before its unsigned loop test. Lengths 1..3 therefore underflow and continue across memory. The candidate intentionally preserves this subtraction/loop behavior; admitted descriptor precondition is zero terminator or nonzero length at least four. Each destination must cover the requested bytes, word-aligned, writable and disjoint from the descriptor stream. Flagged destination encoding uses wrapping uint32 addition with original r9 base, low bit removed. Tested lengths 4..64 include all tail residues, direct and flagged destinations. Descriptor stream/scatter-table integration and producer packing remain unbound; this adapter does not close startup.
