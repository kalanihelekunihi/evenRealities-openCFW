## Correction to earlier finite negative search

The predecessor `../tdk-stock-binding-search-2026-10-09/` correctly retained scope limits, but its zero direct-MOV result must not continue to stand as evidence that mounting or transport is unbound. The positive call/literal bindings above close those specific blockers.

The exact search miss is **symbol-corpus coverage/filtering**, not failure to recognize mov.w. The earlier relaxed query used mnemonic.startswith('mov'), and the earlier strict query explicitly included 'mov.w'. No dedicated symbol row covers `0x507710`. The only containing row is `0x442134..0x5FA132` named FUN_005fa120, with an empty stock_sha256. The earlier scanner skipped hashless rows; linear decoding that giant row also stops at `0x442214`, far before mounting. Thus '0 matching verified symbol ranges' did not inspect this real entrypoint. Auditor's suggestion that mov.w form alone caused the miss is corrected here. Direct target decoding and newly hash-bound intervals are the appropriate evidence.

The previous raw calibration exact-prefix failures are compatible with this newly located *different* 125-byte stock blob. They do not exclude the host GAF path or select 1.1.2. Prior conclusions that float-cache conversion is distinct from int8 public conversion remain valid; its downstream SRAM consumer is now bound.
