# Independent review: audio cache handoff

**Disposition: pass for the borrowed-buffer and checked-policy scope.** This review is bound to `comparison-final-policy.json` SHA-256 `15760d05fd73a68b69819a1630ca7f4c5aa53707793e76ac7f32dd5794b2a0b7` and ELF SHA-256 `5394cbcf100e37c68871e29808f434b06845ee2b5e058f1b3686e3d2c03cdbac`.

The stock query at `0x590B6C` and complete getter at `0x57A7E0` agree with the reconstructed query/getter and the real cache-maintenance provider. The query uses the selector's low byte and the handle's selected-buffer sentinel slots. The getter selects the RX pointer, passes a local `{address, 3200}` range through cache invalidation, and only then publishes address and length. Its R0 return is the selected pointer, matching the stock POP epilogue. There are no executable callee stubs. The fixture's post-query handle mutation is deliberately synthetic and only checks that the selected value was already snapshotted; it is not evidence of an ISR race or hardware schedule.

The added checked adapters are a separate policy layer and are not claimed as stock behavior. `opencfw_cache_checked` rejects missing/unaligned/overflowing allocation bounds, invalid operations, nonpositive signed lengths, and ranges outside the supplied capacity before cache activity. Its range proof accounts for the 32-byte address-block footprint used by the stock loop; it does not establish physical cache geometry, allocation liveness, or exclusive DMA ownership. The checked getter rejects null outputs/handle and, in the final source, identical output pointers before any output publication. Valid success publishes outputs after the cache provider returns; failure leaves the output words untouched. The regular getter retains the observed stock alias-store behavior.

I ran 13 independent native cases against the authenticated stock fragments and final source ELF. They cover low-byte selector behavior, raw getter with cache on/off and synthetic post-query mutation, valid invalidate/clean-invalidate/clean ranges, out-of-bounds rejection, checked getter success, allowed unaligned buffer address inside aligned bounds, identical-output rejection, null handle, and zero capacity. All matched the verifier's semantic/event checks. Results and file hashes are in `representative-rerun.json`.

The 12 huge-length records in `oversized-prefix-results.json` are explicitly `PASS_BOUNDED_PREFIX_ONLY`: both sides reached the 30,000-instruction cap, and no completed huge-positive-length path is claimed. Their prefix evidence is excluded from trace accounting. The standard comparison has 282 original/source cases plus 1,157 checked-policy cases and 160 traced bytes. Of those original bytes, 84 are new after deduplication and 76 overlap earlier evidence. The ledger reconciles to 234 touch + 6,582 Apollo main = 6,816 unique bytes; all four trace-inventory tests pass.

Static call sites show MSPI write calls the clean provider at `0x59CD10`, and the audio RX-buffer helper calls invalidate at `0x57A800`. This establishes code-path use, not display/audio transfer completion or ownership. The getter returns a borrowed pointer and copies no PCM. Audio state mutation after query, live allocation/alignment, DMA coherence, exclusive inactive-buffer status, and physical cache effects remain unproven. The separate ROM40 dependency and optimized-tick instrumentation blocker are unchanged.
