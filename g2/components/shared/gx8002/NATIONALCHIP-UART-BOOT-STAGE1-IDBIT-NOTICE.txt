NationalChip UART boot stage-1 ID-cell and clock-source adaptation
Upstream: NationalChip/lvp_kws arch/soc/grus/include/base_addr.h
(PMU_CFG_SOURCE_SEL0 = GX_REG_BASE_PMU_CONFIG + 0x8C, the clock-source-select
register read by boards/.../clock_board.c)
Commit: 8bf9ee5cb6eeb226011e61c15fa4981b83b93bd5
Local source: runtime_gx8002_uart_stage1_idbit.c
Recovered UART-boot configuration: the stage-1-local ID word at 0x20002008
(chip-revision nibble stored by the early init routine, read here and by the
UART configure routine); the single-bit modifier takes a descriptor whose
word[0] is the bit index and whose byte[4] is the bit value.
Only the numeric register address and the call/return conventions are taken
from the upstream header (used under the SDK repo-level MIT LICENSE below);
all bodies are clean-room implementations of the decoded stock behavior.
The GPL-licensed SDK headers (common.h, spl/spl.h) were deliberately not used.
