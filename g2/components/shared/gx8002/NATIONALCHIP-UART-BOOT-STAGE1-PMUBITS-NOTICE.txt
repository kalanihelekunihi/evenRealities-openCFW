NationalChip UART boot stage-1 PMU trim-bit adaptation
Upstream: NationalChip/lvp_kws arch/soc/grus/include/base_addr.h
Commit: 8bf9ee5cb6eeb226011e61c15fa4981b83b93bd5
Local sources: runtime_gx8002_uart_stage1_pmubits.c,
runtime_gx8002_uart_stage1_pmusbit.S
Recovered UART-boot configuration: PMU_CFG_POWER_ON_RESET_REG1
(GX_REG_BASE_PMU_CONFIG + 0x30, OSC trim state), bit 0 / bit 1 accessors
with a clear, re-read, OR-back access pattern. Only the register address
and bit positions are taken from the upstream header; all bodies are
clean-room implementations of the decoded stock behavior.
