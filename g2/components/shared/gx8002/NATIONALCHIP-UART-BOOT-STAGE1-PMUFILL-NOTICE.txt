Upstream: NationalChip/lvp_kws arch/soc/grus/include/base_addr.h
(GX_REG_BASE_MCU_CONFIG = 0xA0300000 with MCU_CFG_SOURCE_SEL (+0x88) and
the MCU_CFG_MEPG_CLK_INHIBIT_NORM/1SET/1CLR triple (+0x18/+0x1C/+0x20);

Commit: 8bf9ee5cb6eeb226011e61c15fa4981b83b93bd5
Local source: runtime_gx8002_uart_stage1_pmufill.c
Recovered UART-boot configuration: the 26-entry peripheral table at
0x20002018 (16-byte entries, first word matched against the id) and the
id < 10 MCU/PMU domain select are decoded stock behavior, not upstream.
Only the numeric register bases and the call/return conventions are taken
from the upstream header (used under the SDK repo-level MIT LICENSE below);
all bodies are clean-room implementations of the decoded stock behavior.
The GPL-licensed SDK headers (common.h, spl/spl.h) were deliberately not used.
