## Key specifications (DS, QMA6100P)

3-axis accelerometer, 14-bit ADC, I2C/SPI, 12-pin LGA 2 × 2 × 0.95 mm,
−40 to +85 °C; step counting and tilt features.

## Relevance to decompilation

register map is documented in licensed third-party drivers named in the
