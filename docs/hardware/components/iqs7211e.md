## Key specifications (DS)

Trackpad/touchscreen controller, 1–2 finger tracking, reports up to 100 Hz,
low-power proximity/wake; I2C up to 1 MHz; QFN20 (13 sensor pads) or WLCSP18
(11 pads).

## R1 use (FW)

TWIM0 400 kHz, SCL P0.12, SDA P0.01, LDO enable P0.30, RDY/MCLR P0.17
(active-low, falling-edge event).

## Relevance to decompilation

Settings blob and register-word accesses match Azoteq's settings template
format (lineage references in the boundary document).
